Privacy Policy

Short version: we collect nothing, because there is nowhere for it to go.

Bunko eReader is an EPUB reading app that runs entirely on your device. It requires no internet connection, no account, and no ad or analytics service. The books you read and your reading progress are stored only on your own phone — this information is never shared with us or anyone else.

01 Data We Collect

Bunko eReader does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data from you or your device to any server. This app has no server, no database, and no backend.

Name, email, phone Location data Usage analytics Advertising ID Crash reporting Account / sign-in

None of the items above are collected by the app — no third-party SDK (advertising, analytics, crash tracking, etc.) is included in the app.

02 Data Stored on Your Device

For the app to work properly, the following information is kept only on your own device, in a local storage file:

  • Reading progress, such as the page/chapter you left off on
  • Your reader preferences, such as font size and theme
  • A pagination cache kept so pages open faster

This data never leaves your device; it is not sent over the internet, backed up, or shared with us.

03 File Access Permission

The app requests access to your device's file system so it can open the EPUB files you choose. This permission is used only to read the files you select from within the app. Your book files are read, processed, and stay on your device — they are never transmitted outside the app.

04 Internet and Third Parties

Bunko eReader is designed to work entirely offline. The app does not include any advertising network, analytics library, crash-reporting service, or other third-party service that collects data. As a result, your data is never shared with or sold to any third party.

05 Children's Privacy

Because the app does not collect personal data from anyone — including children — it is safe for users of all ages. There is no separate data-handling process for users under 13, since no data is processed in the first place.

06 Deleting Your Data

Since all reading data is kept only on your device, it remains entirely under your control. You can permanently delete all local data by clearing the app's storage or by uninstalling the app.

07 Changes to This Policy

This privacy policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page, and the "last updated" date at the top will be revised accordingly.

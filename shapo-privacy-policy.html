Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 25, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Shapo, a puzzle game developed by Furkan Akif Islek (“we”, “us” or “our”), handles information when you use the mobile app.

You can play without creating an account. Your puzzle progress and preferences are saved on your device. The mobile app uses Google Analytics for Firebase to help us understand app usage.

1. Information stored on your device

Shapo saves completed levels, unfinished puzzles, piece positions, elapsed play time, hint counts and preferences such as language, music, sound, haptics and piece symbols in local app storage. This allows you to resume play and keep your settings. The current app does not upload these game saves to a developer-operated cloud account.

Your operating system may include app data in device backups, depending on your device and backup settings. Those backups are managed by your platform provider.

2. Analytics information

On Android and iOS, Shapo uses Google Analytics for Firebase. Analytics collection is enabled when the mobile app starts. We record app openings and screen views; the SDK also collects automatic usage events and technical information.

This information can include app-instance identifiers, app version, device model, operating system, language, session activity, interactions and approximate location derived from network information. Depending on the platform, SDK configuration and device permissions, the SDK may also process advertising identifiers. Shapo does not request precise GPS location or send your name or email address as an analytics user ID.

We use analytics to understand usage patterns and improve the game. The current app does not offer an in-app analytics opt-out switch.

3. Store update checks

Shapo checks its store listing for newer app versions. These requests are sent to the relevant store service and may include the app identifier and store or locale information. As with other internet requests, the receiving service can process connection information such as your IP address.

4. Information you provide when contacting us

If you contact us, we receive the information you choose to send, such as your email address and message. We use it to respond to your request and resolve the issue. Please avoid sending sensitive personal information.

5. Sharing and third-party services

Google processes analytics information as the provider of Google Analytics for Firebase. Apple or Google may process information when the app checks store listings or you open a store page. These providers handle information under their own terms and privacy policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Privacy and Security in Firebase
  • Apple Privacy Policy

We do not sell personal information. The current version does not include third-party advertising, account registration or in-app purchases. Information may also be disclosed where required by law or necessary to protect rights, security or safety.

6. Retention and deletion

Local progress and preferences remain on your device until removed through app storage controls or uninstallation, subject to your platform’s backup and restore behavior. On Android, you can clear Shapo’s app data in system settings. Removing local data does not delete analytics information already processed by Google.

Analytics retention is controlled by the settings of the Google Analytics property and Google’s applicable policies. This policy does not specify a fixed retention period because it depends on that configuration. Contact us about a privacy or deletion request; our ability to locate particular analytics records may be limited because Shapo does not require a personal account.

7. Your privacy choices and rights

You can manage available device privacy and advertising settings and remove locally stored app data. Device advertising controls do not necessarily stop all analytics collection. Depending on where you live, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion or restriction of personal information, object to certain processing, or complain to your local data protection authority. Contact us to make a request.

8. Children’s privacy

Shapo does not ask players to provide their name, age, email address or other contact details to play. The analytics processing described above can still occur when the app is used. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so we can review and address the request.

9. International processing and security

Third-party providers may process information in countries other than your country of residence. We use reasonable safeguards appropriate to the information we handle, but no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as Shapo or its data practices change. We will publish the revised policy on this page and update the date above.
